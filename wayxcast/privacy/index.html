WayXCast privacy policy

Effective 30 October 2026.

What stays on your phone

Your rides (route, times, speeds and altitude), ride videos, slideshow photos, settings and a small event log are kept only on your phone. They are removed when you delete them or remove WayXCast.

What leaves your phone

Your approximate location (rounded to about 2 km) goes to the weather service: Apple Weather on iPhone, MET Norway (the Norwegian Meteorological Institute) on Android. The map service (OpenFreeMap with OpenStreetMap data on Android, Apple Maps on iPhone) receives the map area being shown. WayXCast downloads BMKG's public earthquake feed without sending anything. The bike display picture goes only to your motorcycle over its Wi-Fi.

Permissions and why

Location: recording rides and local weather, daylight and prayer times. Photos and videos: ride moments, the slideshow and saving ride videos. Calendar and Health: only if you turn those topics on, read-only, and never sent anywhere. Nearby devices or local network: connecting to your motorcycle's display. Notifications: showing that a ride is recording.

Purchases

WayXCast Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play. WayXCast only learns whether you own Pro; it never sees your payment details.

Sharing

Nothing is shared unless you choose to: GPX files, ride videos and the diagnostics report go only where you send them. The diagnostics report contains no location and masks network addresses.

No accounts, ads or tracking

WayXCast has no account, no ads and no analytics, and your data is never sold.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date below shows the current version. Contact SDMV at hello@sdmv.my.id. Effective 30 October 2026.

Kebijakan privasi WayXCast

Berlaku sejak 30 October 2026.

Yang tetap di HP kamu

Riding kamu (rute, waktu, kecepatan, dan ketinggian), video riding, foto slideshow, pengaturan, dan log kejadian kecil hanya disimpan di HP kamu. Semuanya terhapus saat kamu menghapusnya atau menghapus WayXCast.

Yang dikirim dari HP kamu

Perkiraan lokasi kamu (dibulatkan sekitar 2 km) dikirim ke layanan cuaca: Apple Weather di iPhone, MET Norway (Institut Meteorologi Norwegia) di Android. Layanan peta (OpenFreeMap dengan data OpenStreetMap di Android, Apple Maps di iPhone) menerima area peta yang ditampilkan. WayXCast mengunduh data gempa publik BMKG tanpa mengirim apa pun. Gambar layar motor hanya dikirim ke motor kamu lewat Wi-Fi motor.

Izin dan alasannya

Lokasi: merekam riding serta cuaca, waktu terang, dan jadwal salat setempat. Foto dan video: momen riding, slideshow, dan menyimpan video riding. Kalender dan Kesehatan: hanya jika topiknya kamu aktifkan, hanya dibaca, dan tidak pernah dikirim ke mana pun. Perangkat sekitar atau jaringan lokal: menghubungkan ke layar motor. Notifikasi: menunjukkan riding sedang direkam.

Pembelian

WayXCast Pro dibeli lewat App Store atau Google Play. WayXCast hanya tahu apakah kamu punya Pro; detail pembayaran kamu tidak pernah terlihat.

Berbagi

Tidak ada yang dibagikan kecuali kamu memilihnya: file GPX, video riding, dan laporan diagnostik hanya dikirim ke tujuan yang kamu pilih. Laporan diagnostik tidak berisi lokasi dan menyamarkan alamat jaringan.

Tanpa akun, iklan, atau pelacakan

WayXCast tidak punya akun, iklan, maupun analitik, dan data kamu tidak pernah dijual.
