WayXCast

Your ride, on your bike's display.

WayXCast turns your motorcycle's display into a ride companion, records every ride, and makes a video of it to share. It works with compatible motorcycle displays over the bike's Wi-Fi.

Free and Pro Support
Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

Android and iPhone. English and Bahasa Indonesia.

  • A ride companion on the bike display

    Weather, rain alerts, air quality, sunset, prayer times, earthquake alerts and your trip, drawn for the wide bike display and easy to read at a glance.

  • Every ride recorded

    Route, distance, riding time, speed and climb. Turn on automatic rides and a ride starts when you ride off and ends when you park. Export any ride as GPX.

  • Ride videos

    A 3D flyover of your route with the photos and clips you took along the way, ready to share.

  • Your photos on the bike

    A slideshow of your photos and GIFs on the bike display, framed around faces so nobody gets cut off.

  • Screen sharing

    Show your phone's screen on the bike display when you want to.

Over the bike's Wi-Fi

Works with compatible motorcycle displays. No account, no sign-in and no Bluetooth key.

Stays on your phone

No ads or tracking. Your rides, photos and videos stay on your phone unless you share them.

Set up while parked

Once it's connected, the bike display is for quick glances. Your phone can stay in your pocket.

Free to ride. Pro if you want more.

Riding, recording and the ride companion are free. WayXCast Pro is a one-time purchase through the App Store or Google Play, with no subscription.

Free

Everything you need to ride

  • Rides and automatic rides

    No limit on rides, with the ride log and GPX export.

  • The ride companion

    Up to 6 topics on the bike display at once, plus earthquake alerts.

  • Slideshow and screen sharing

    A slideshow of 10 photos, and screen sharing for 15 minutes at a time.

  • Ride videos up to 1080p

    Up to 60 seconds, with up to 8 photos and clips.

No ads, no account

WayXCast Pro

One purchase, yours to keep

  • More on your bike display

    Every topic at once, with Health, your next calendar event and weekly milestones.

  • A bigger slideshow, no sharing limit

    Up to 40 photos and GIFs, and screen sharing for as long as you ride.

  • Sharper, longer ride videos

    1440p and Best quality, videos up to 90 seconds, with up to 20 photos and clips.

  • Display colours and a badge

    Five more bike display colours and a Supporter badge in the app.

One-time purchase, no subscription

Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play, and their refund rules apply. Restore purchase in the app brings Pro back on a new phone with the same Apple Account or Google account.
