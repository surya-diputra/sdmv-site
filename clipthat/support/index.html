ClipThat Studio Support

How can we help?

Answers to the common questions are below. If something still isn't working, email us and we'll get back to you.

hello@sdmv.my.id

Tell us your device model, the app version (shown in About) and what you see.

Where are my videos and projects kept?

On your device, or on the external drive or folder you choose under Storage on the Home screen. There's no account and no cloud copy, so nothing is lost or synced through us.

If the drive is unplugged or full, ClipThat Studio tells you and lets you plug it back in or switch to the device's own storage. Keep a backup of anything important: removing the app removes what's stored on the device.

Captions don't appear

Captions are transcribed on your device, so the device's speech recognition has to support your language.

  • iPhone, iPad and Mac: allow Speech Recognition for ClipThat Studio in Settings if you said no earlier. The first time, your device may need a moment to get the language ready.
  • Android: if your phone has no on-device speech recognizer, ClipThat Studio offers to download an offline captions model (about 60 MB) once. It needs an internet connection for that download only.

Clear speech with little background music gives the best captions.

How do I film with a crew?

One device directs and up to four others film. All of them need ClipThat Studio.

  1. On the Director's device, tap Direct a crew. It shows a QR code.
  2. On each camera, tap Join a crew and scan the code.
  3. Allow Local Network (iPhone, iPad and Mac) or Nearby devices (Android) when asked.

The devices talk directly over your Wi-Fi, encrypted, never through a server. With no Wi-Fi around, an Android device can make a network for the crew, or an iPhone can share its Personal Hotspot. Takes go straight to the Director's timeline.

Can't connect? Make sure every device is on the same Wi-Fi, and that guest or public networks aren't blocking devices from seeing each other.

The Director can't see a camera's videos

The Director can browse videos already on a camera only after the person on that camera taps Allow, and only for that connection. If they tapped Don't allow, reconnect and ask again.

The camera also needs photo library access for ClipThat Studio in its Settings. On Android, if only some videos were allowed, the Director sees only those.

Setting up the AI Director

The AI Director turns your idea into a shot plan. Choose one way to use it:

  • Your own API key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, DeepSeek or another compatible provider. The key stays in your device's Keychain or Keystore, and the provider bills you for what you use.
  • A chat app: copy the prompt into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, then bring the answer back. No key needed.
  • Apple Intelligence on supported iPhone, iPad and Mac, on the device. No key needed.

Your idea and plan text go only to the provider you choose, never to us. See the Privacy Policy.

Sending a plan to another device

On the other device, open Plans, tap ⋯ and choose Receive a plan. On your device, open the plan and choose Send to nearby device, then pick the other device from the list. If it doesn't appear, tap Scan code and scan the QR code it shows. Both devices need to be on the same network, and the other person taps Accept.

Plans are sent unencrypted over the local network, so send them only on networks you trust. You can also use Share plan… to send the plan as a file, for example with AirDrop or Quick Share.
