ClipThat Studio

Plan it, film it, edit it.

ClipThat Studio is a video editor for short videos. Plan your shots, film them with a crew of phones over your own Wi-Fi, then edit with captions, voiceovers, looks and text.

Support
Coming soon

iPhone, iPad and Android, plus ClipThat Studio for Mac. English.

  • Plans and the AI Director

    Turn an idea into a shot list with the AI provider you choose, then film shot by shot with the plan on screen.

  • Crew

    One device directs and up to four cameras film, linked directly over your Wi-Fi. Takes land on the Director's timeline.

  • Captions and voiceovers

    Captions are transcribed on your device. Record voiceovers right on the timeline.

  • Looks and text

    28 colour looks, 4 effects, 18 fonts and 22 text styles, with export settings made for Reels.

No account

No sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Stays on your device

Your videos, plans and captions stay on your device or the drive you choose, unless you share them.

Your own Wi-Fi

Crew devices talk to each other directly over the local network, never through a server.
