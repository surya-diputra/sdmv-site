How it works

One idea. One app. Your whole film crew.

1Plan

Every shot, mapped out.

Describe the video you want. The AI Director turns it into a shot plan: framing, movement, timing and lines.

  • Use any AI chat assistant: the app writes the prompt, you paste the reply back.
  • Or use your own Anthropic or OpenAI-compatible key, or Apple's on-device model.

1Plan

A storyboard you can play.

Plans are storyboards that live apart from your projects. Start from a video type, sketch the shot you want, record a voiceover for each one, then play the plan like a video.

  • Board and list views, and a shot editor for every detail.
  • Send a plan as a file, or over Wi-Fi to a nearby device.

2Film

Film it shot by shot.

The shot-card camera shows what to film next, with live coaching, and every take lands in the right place in your plan.

2Film

Roll every camera from one.

Friends' phones become your camera crew. The Director sees every camera live, rolls and cuts them all with matched settings, and the takes arrive in original quality.

3Edit

A real timeline in your pocket.

Trim, split and reorder. Stack as many video, photo, music, sound and voiceover lines as you need. Every edit saves itself, and any step can be undone.

  • Crew takes line up as a synced multicam edit.
  • Social preview shows where Reels, TikTok and Shorts cover your video.

3Edit

Ready for Reels.

Captions from your voice, a voiceover after a countdown, looks and text. Export to your photos at the settings made for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, with captions and text burned in.

Questions

Is ClipThat Studio free?

Yes. The editor, captions, voiceover, plans, the AI Director, looks and full-quality export are free, with no watermark. Pro is a one-time purchase for bigger crews, the word-by-word caption looks, all 12 video types and the Pro AI features. There's no subscription. The price is shown in your app store.

One App Store purchase covers your iPhone and iPad; Google Play is a separate purchase. On the Mac, everything is free.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up. AI features use your own key or, on the Mac, your own Claude account.

Which languages do captions support?

Captions are made from speech on your device in English and Bahasa Indonesia, and you can translate them between the two with AI assist.

Can iPhone and Android phones film together?

Yes. iPhone, iPad and Android phones running ClipThat Studio join one crew on the same local network, and a Mac can be the Director. A free Director takes one camera; Pro takes up to four.

Where are my videos kept?

On your device, or on an external drive if you choose one. The app tells you when the drive is missing or full. Crew takes travel over your local network, from camera to Director.

What do the AI features send, and where?

Only text (your brief, plan and captions), never video or audio, and only to the service you pick and confirm. Review my edit and Smart rough cut also send small still frames, only after you allow it. Any cost of using your AI provider is between you and that provider.
