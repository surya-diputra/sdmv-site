AutoMaxi and WayXAuto

Android Auto, on the dash you already have.

Two free, ad-free apps that send Android Auto from your phone to your motorcycle's built-in screen. Set up once, then connect and ride.

AutoMaxi

For Yamaha XMAX and NMAX

Streams the Android Auto map to the XMAX or NMAX instrument cluster and keeps it running while you ride.

  • One-button connect

    Tap Connect once. AutoMaxi finds your cluster, pairs and streams until you stop it.

  • Runs in the background

    Keeps streaming with the phone screen off, so your map stays live for the whole ride.

  • Built for the cluster

    The map fits the XMAX and NMAX dash, not a phone screen squeezed onto it.

Set up in three steps

  1. Install AutoMaxi. Download the APK and allow the install from your browser or files app.
  2. Import the keys and enable Android Auto developer mode. The app links you to the free public Open Headunit keys and shows you how.
  3. Pair your cluster, then tap Connect. Pair the XMAX or NMAX in Bluetooth settings first.
Download AutoMaxi

WayXAuto

For OMOWAY displays

Runs Android Auto on your phone and shows it on the OMOWAY navigation screen over Wi-Fi, with maps and music controlled from your phone.

  • Wi-Fi, no pairing fuss

    Turn on your motorcycle, tap Find and choose its display. No account and no Bluetooth key.

  • Live preview on your phone

    See exactly what your dash shows before you set off.

  • Fits the display

    Laid out for the wide OMOWAY screen, clear of its clock and overlays.

Set up in three steps

  1. Install WayXAuto. Download the APK and allow the install from your browser or files app.
  2. Import the keys and turn on Android Auto developer mode. WayXAuto downloads the free public Open Headunit keys for you.
  3. Turn on your motorcycle and pick its display. Tap Find, choose your bike and Android Auto appears on the dash.
Download WayXAuto

AutoMaxi and WayXAuto are experimental, independent projects, not affiliated with Yamaha, OMOWAY or Google. Set everything up before you ride, and never use your phone or dash controls while riding.

Fake GPS - Inertia Joystick

Mock locations that move like a real vehicle.

Most location spoofers teleport or crawl at one robotic speed. This joystick runs on a physics engine, so it speeds up, glides and brakes naturally.

Get it on Google Play

Android. No root needed.

  • Inertia joystick

    Real acceleration, momentum and braking for natural movement.

  • Your own speed profile

    Tune the physics for walking, cycling, riding or driving.

  • Floating 360° joystick

    Works over your other apps, with a small map monitor.

  • Bookmarks

    Set a start point in one tap and save the places you use most.

Set it up

  1. In Settings, open About phone and tap Build number seven times.
  2. Open Developer options and find Select mock location app.
  3. Choose Fake GPS - Inertia Joystick.
  4. Open the app, pick a spot on the map and start the joystick.

SDMV EngineSound

Give your electric bike an engine.

EngineSound synthesizes a combustion engine live on your phone. The revs follow your GPS speed through a virtual gearbox, so it pulls through the gears as you ride away and settles when you cruise. There are no sound recordings in the app: every note is made as you ride.

App support

Android and iOS. Coming soon.

  • Ride mode

    Your GPS speed drives an automatic gearbox that changes up as you pull away and drops the revs when you hold a steady speed.

  • Rev mode

    Throttle and brake sliders and shift buttons, with each vehicle's real top speed and gearing.

  • Thirty engines

    Ten motorcycles, ten cars and ten futuristic electric drives, from a thumper single to a V12.

  • Plays like music

    Keeps going in the background with the screen off. Control it from the lock screen, the notification or your headset.

Privacy Policy

SDMV DashConnect. Last Updated: June 21, 2026

At SDMV, we are fully committed to protecting your security. This Privacy Policy details the processes, permissions, and data safety practices of the SDMV mobile application.

1. Core App Permissions

Because SDMV acts as a local telemetry gauge, specific permissions are required strictly to perform on-device updates. None of this data is collected, stored, or sent to remote servers:

  • Location Information (GPS): Necessary to calculate vehicle speeds, duration logs, and drag timer metrics.
  • Foreground Services: Used to maintain calculations if the phone is placed in a pocket during active riding.

2. Advertisements (Google AdMob)

This app displays ads managed by Google AdMob. AdMob uses unique advertising identifiers (such as the device's Google Advertising ID) to deliver relevant promotions. No identifiable personal profiles are compiled.

3. Information Storage and Deletion

Because SDMV does not use remote databases to store your riding records, all configurations and data logs can be instantly wiped by selecting "Clear Data" under Android operating system settings, or by simply uninstalling the app.

For questions regarding the application, please contact support directly at: support@sdmv.site.
